RGC Technologies - Company Overview
RGC Technologies Limited is an Irish company that provides elite Computerised Maintenance Management System (CMMS) solutions.
Our flagship product is the RGC Enterprise System, also known as JOBPAL.
We empower organisations worldwide to achieve unparalleled excellence in maintenance and asset management.

Key Products & Services:
1. RGC Enterprise System (CMMS / CAFM):
   - A comprehensive system to streamline maintenance operations.
   - Gives clients full visibility of activities to maximise ROI and minimise unexpected downtime.
   - Supports multiple companies on one database.

2. Mobile App for Engineers:
   - Allows field and internal staff to pro-actively manage tasks in real-time.

3. Asset & Facilities Management:
   - Allocate and manage jobs to ensure assets are tracked and facilities remain operational.

4. Preventative and Reactive Maintenance:
   - Maximise ROI by managing uptime and minimising costly downtime.
   - Supports Planned, Preventative and Predictive (PPM) maintenance scheduling.

5. Stock Control:
   - Optimise supply chain with visibility of inventory and stock across multiple sites and warehouses.

6. Project Management:
   - Manage projects, cost estimates, timescales, and progress from one system.

7. Reporting:
   - Reporting tool provides visibility of key information to manage the business.
